Component Descriptions

RBF (Long-term Trend)

Captures gradual changes in baseline demand — e.g., new store openings or shifting consumer habits in Binh Thuan. Points close in time are highly correlated; distant points have exponentially decaying correlation.

ExpSineSquared (Weekly Cycle)

Models recurring demand spikes — higher weekend sales of vegetables, meat and seafood at local markets. Periodicity p=7 captures the standard weekly buying cycle.

WhiteKernel (Noise)

Accounts for unpredictable variation — sudden coastal storms, supply disruptions, measurement error. δᵢⱼ = 1 only when i = j (diagonal noise).

Model Requirements

Minimum 14 data points (2 full weeks) required for reliable fitting. Optimal performance with ≥ 60 data points (2 months). Parameters are automatically optimized via log-marginal-likelihood maximization.
